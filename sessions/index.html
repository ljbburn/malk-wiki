Session Log

07 Oct 20264 min read

Session numbers follow the DM’s campaign record. On 1 October 2026 every session was renumbered +5 to count the five Daggerford sessions at the start. Older notes may use the old numbers.

The first ten sessions, with no recordings, are gathered on early-sessions. Sessions with a full page: 25 · 26 · 27.

Daggerford

#Session
1–5Daggerford. The party gathers in Daggerford and takes a contract escorting Priya Nagorth’s wagon. No recordings or notes beyond the map and the caravan-guards poster in the gallery. See early-sessions.

Bearbarrow and the road south

#Session
6–7Bearbarrow. Something escapes Priya’s wagon and replaces the stable hand Julius: an oblex. The party exposes it, clears the mine below the village, and finds a strongbox marked WSS.
8Road South. A carnival; the fortune-teller Zivra delivers three prophecies.
9Fields of the Dead. A wyvern fight; a griffon at an old watchtower; the Griffontack awakens.
10Eladrin Ruins. Kaelith finds a crossing point toward Cendriane. In a drake ambush a thief steals the strongbox. Cassia briefs the party on the WSS; a letter from Aldren Thace.

Baldur’s Gate

#Session
11Arrival in Baldur’s Gate. Theron Vale’s reward poster for Odettia. Shopping at the Basilisk Gate; Thace’s letter delivered to the Shrine of the Moonmaiden. Odettia confronts Theron in disguise.
12Investigation. Leads from Harlan at the Loose Rigger about the Tideworth; a fight at the Vellum Stage; the party meets Milo and Neve Saren and overhears the name “Holloway”.
13Three Heartbeats. At the Closed Door: Sundar sold the key, the buyer’s agent was “Mourne”, and a letter signed “M.D.” Odettia fights the Black Boars. Vander sets the stew record at the Lizard’s Cradle (8 bowls). Dreams.
14The Last Day. Spying on Theron as the Black Boars lean on him; the wizard Edren Gale teleports to the Boars’ hideout. The party clears a jeweller’s cellar connected to the sewers and plants a false trail.
15Sail-Off. Thalwick’s book bought. Milo’s mother Marloes freed from her Guild debt (Odettia signs as “Morgan Duskbane”). A coastal chart; a vision at Luna’s shrine. The Maris sails at dawn.

The voyage

#Session
16Storm at Sea. Cassia’s letter narrows “Aldric Mourne” to four men, including the brothers Erstin and Renard Holloway. Kaelith’s fading surges and her emerald crumbles, stopping it. Neve goes overboard and is rescued. Odettia’s wings become common knowledge.
17Raiders in the Dark. A night sahuagin raid; Milo’s first kill; spiderweb and fish-head coins; a fog bank ahead.
18The Chart and the Drift. The chart is burned and the compass fouled by an unknown saboteur. Quinn is found stowed away in the hold. Reza Sahil’s three contests (a drawn duel, Biscuit befriended, cards won); a replacement chart and the greatsword Marianne gained. Adrift.
19The Wreck on the Sandbank. A planted lodestone removed from the compass. The coins lead to a drowned Waterdeep navy wreck; its dead crew fought. A ship’s log, a cap of water breathing, and Bran’s carvings found aboard. A kraken spawn surfaces.
20The Dark Beacon. Escape from the kraken spawn. Crossing to dark Viledel; an allip destroyed.
21Lighthouse Interiors. A dread wight and a dread wraith destroyed; the beacon relit. Quinn looks into the orb and is cursed. A keeper’s diary mentions the Tideworth.
22Underway. Two days’ crossing from Viledel to Saltmere.

Saltmere

#Session
23The Better Man. Docking in Saltmere (a blank permit gets them past the harbourmaster). Neve’s debt revealed. Errands at the market, chapel, forge, jeweller and healer. News of the new governor, Leon Thrace, and the old governor’s funeral; the party splits three ways.
24A Tomorrow Worth Trusting. Odettia runs the funeral from the inside. Quinn and Milo infiltrate the manor and find a hidden dungeon with fifteen prisoners. Vander and Kaelith destroy the storehouse guardian.
25**The Ones Who Never Left.** Escape to the Green Leaf; Luna’s mount form; four solo jobs around town; the Isolde vision, Cole’s cabinet, Harlan risen, Corv; Leon’s invitation.
26**The Coven.** Familiars spot the coven; a fight in the woods at Harlan’s farm; Osric rescued; the lair beneath the farm; a guarded truce.
27**The Governor’s Table.** Laura’s confession; Odettia’s bite; dinner with Leon and his offer; Quinn meets Isolde; the vial points beneath the manor.
