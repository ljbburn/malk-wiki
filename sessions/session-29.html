Session 29: Before First Light

Session 29: Before First Light

24 Oct 20266 min read

Previous: Session 28 · Next: Session 30

Fog and fire

The fight beneath Saltmere’s manor resumed with Osric bound and gagged in the cart. Odettia cast Fog Cloud, humming as mist poured from her hand and swallowed the corridor. The guards lost their bearings and fired blindly: one bolt wounded Osric, another struck a fellow guard, and a later shot narrowly missed Kaelith. [00:00:22–00:02:58; 00:17:25–00:19:39]

Kaelith cast Flaming Sphere to free the prisoners from the tendrils. With careful control she guided it through the bars, burning the strands without striking the captives. A prisoner remembered his daughter’s laughter; others recovered the sound of a wife’s voice and a father’s stories. The shrieks from beyond the closed door grew louder as more connections were severed. [00:06:44–00:14:24; 00:35:37–00:37:24; 00:56:20]

Vander charged into the fog. Selûne offered to be his eyes: his blade and eyes shone, and the fog lifted from his sight. He killed one guard. Quinn entered the mist and killed the other nearby guard with his psychic blades. Odettia’s imitation of a guard’s voice failed to obtain the cell keys, but her Healing Hands restored five hit points to Osric. [00:26:45–00:34:49; 00:41:36–00:45:06; 00:51:51–00:52:24]

Eight escaped, four died

Vander severed another strand with Sunbolt. Then the remaining tendrils changed: the wisps became solid, wrapped around four prisoners’ necks and killed them. Kaelith hurled her sphere at the withdrawing connection and cut it off; the detached material fell and writhed on the floor. [01:00:45–01:05:12]

The party opened the surviving captives’ cells. Quinn picked locks, Odettia melted three locks with phoenix-shaped fire, and Kaelith burned through the locks holding two elven twins. Vander guided a confused elderly man through the fog. Eight prisoners escaped; four died. [01:03:31–01:09:40; 01:20:43–01:21:40]

Odettia worried about who they were releasing. The elderly man’s remarks about his business partner troubled her, and a young woman tried to pick her pocket. She raised an illusory wall and questioned the prisoners, with Quinn coming to help. A frightened boy, Brennan, said he had been falsely accused of theft and brought there by boat. He appeared sincere, and Odettia sent her owl to guide him out. When the young pickpocket discovered the wall was an illusion, the others rushed through. [01:09:58–01:20:12]

The cart and the papers

Searching the dead guards, Vander found one key, thirty gold pieces and a charge sheet for Halflin, one of the elven twins. It listed numerous alleged offences and an extraction scheduled for that day. [01:22:46–01:25:25]

Odettia recognised the Gundwynd crest on Osric’s cart. It was a shipping container fitted with wheels and reinforced to hold something trying to escape; inside were bloodstains and scratches. A paper pinned to Osric listed alleged crimes including unlawful investigation and plucking a sunflower from the governor’s garden. The papers recorded accusations, not proof of every charge. [01:25:36–01:29:36]

Corv’s vial strained toward the reinforced door. The missing ingredient was still somewhere beyond their reach. [01:21:53–01:22:46]

Captain Voss’s warning

Luna healed Kaelith before the party approached the door. The guard’s key was too small. When Kaelith tried to burn through the door, ice crept from the other side and resisted her fire. A voice recognised as Captain Voss, captain of Leon’s guard, told them to take Osric and the prisoners and leave. Odettia challenged him; he invoked the governor’s authority and warned that their deaths would not be his responsibility. [01:31:34–01:37:05; 01:41:15–01:41:49]

Quinn discovered a fist-sized ventilation opening, climbed up and looked through. He reported four people and an unidentified mist beyond the door. Kaelith’s ritual detected a mage using ice magic there. What produced the tendrils remained unidentified. [01:43:05–01:45:29; 01:56:06–01:56:49; 02:09:27–02:09:32]

Voss rang an alarm and warned that the governor’s battalion would arrive in about two minutes. His warning, and his reluctance to kill them, seemed sincere. More bells answered from above. [01:57:19–02:00:57]

The forced retreat

Kaelith still wanted to reach the room and the remedy ingredient. As the others prepared to flee, Odettia struck her on the back of the head with her dagger’s handle, knocking her unconscious. Vander loaded Kaelith onto Luna beside Osric. Odettia used Celestial Revelation to fly. [02:01:04–02:02:16]

Odettia’s owl had seen the escaping prisoners reach an unguarded warehouse route. The party followed it and burst into the quiet harbour while alarms and torches spread around the manor above. They headed toward Harlan’s farm. Vander gave Brennan money for a room, and Odettia guided him to the Green Leaf, using her owl to alert Milo as well. Exhaustion caught up with everyone as dawn approached. [02:02:16–02:08:24]

A patrol stopped them on the road. Vander bluffed that they had been sent to seek support, and the guards let them pass. Quinn remained hidden and stole twenty gold pieces from one of them. [02:10:32–02:13:56]

Repent and make right

Outside town, Vander’s oath burned in his thoughts. He had freed people who prey on the weak. Selûne told him to repent and make things right, and Luna vanished, dropping Osric and Kaelith. Kaelith woke from the fall. Vander resolved to deal with the dangerous escapees after the party had rested; Halflin’s charge sheet remained in his possession. [02:15:10–02:21:36; 02:25:05–02:26:31]

Odettia examined the still-unconscious Osric and noticed sickly-sweet breath. Drugging or poisoning was suspected, not confirmed. She explained to Kaelith why she had forced the retreat, and Kaelith healed herself. [02:22:06–02:24:14]

Milo caught up and offered fifty gold from his tavern winnings. At the deserted farmhouse, Vander tried to imbue his greatsword with Selûne’s light, but it remained cold steel. His learned spells remained available; Luna and the Selûne-linked weapon blessing were lost. [02:26:39–02:30:38; 02:38:39–02:40:10]

Milo performed a fiery working and said he had set a hellish rebuke at the front door. Odettia left a warning to keep five feet away and a request to talk to the coven later. Milo marked the exclusion area in chalk. Quinn smelled blood toward the cellar but left it for after sleep. At about five in the morning, the party settled down. [02:31:10–02:37:03]

Where things stand

  • Osric is rescued but unconscious, with the cause of his condition unresolved.
  • Eight prisoners escaped and four died. Halflin, an elf and one of the twins, is among the escapees; Vander holds his sheet.
  • The reinforced door remains unopened. The mist, the mage’s identity, the tendrils’ source and Corv’s missing ingredient remain unresolved.
  • Saltmere is under alarm and lockdown. Another approach to the manor will be harder.
  • Vander needs to make amends and restore his bond with Luna and Selûne’s blessing; his shoulder injury also remains untreated.
  • The coven meeting, the blood scent in the cellar and the outcome of Sibbet’s before-dawn disposal order remain open. The party has settled to rest; a completed long rest has not yet been played.
