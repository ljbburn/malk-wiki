Session 30: Until Midnight, I Am Yours

Session 30: Until Midnight, I Am Yours

08 Nov 20267 min read

Previous: Session 29 · Next: Session 31

The rest and the unraveling

The party rested at Harlan’s farmhouse, with the long rest expected to end around one in the afternoon. Vander played a sorrowful but determined tune on his pan flute. Odettia’s bite was worsening: black, web-like veins spread from the wound, her neck tightened and the promise of power clouded her thoughts. She counted two days remaining. Looking out after sleeping, she saw a thin plume of smoke along the cliff, but its source was not investigated. [00:00:06–00:05:27]

Kaelith’s left hand and forearm vanished up to the elbow. This time there was no translucent outline, and the limb did not return. She screamed. She could feel phantom movement, but could not make contact with the missing hand. [00:09:22–00:10:59; 00:15:08–00:15:39; 00:18:06–00:19:21; 00:24:25–00:24:33]

Odettia cast Identify and explained the result: Temporal Refuge, a spell cast by two people to bind someone in time, was unraveling. Its deterioration was tearing Kaelith’s body apart. It would not take her home. A taste of Corv’s unfinished remedy had no effect, and changing into a bear gave Kaelith only three legs. She still intended to finish the remedy for Corv first and learn how to make another; its final ingredient remained unrecovered. [00:11:01–00:11:12; 00:19:21–00:20:06; 00:21:38–00:25:17]

Quinn resisted another encroachment from the orb curse. Kaelith’s Pot of Awakening made a sound and developed cracks, but the shrub was not yet fully awakened. Milo asked his mysterious father about her missing arm and could offer no solution. [00:26:16–00:27:03; 00:33:01–00:36:32; 00:43:37–00:44:42]

Osric wakes

Odettia identified the sweet-smelling poison keeping Osric unconscious. Vander used Lay on Hands to remove it. Green vapour escaped Osric’s mouth, and he woke nauseated and asking for water. Vander went to the well. The magic worked, but he described it as hollow without his familiar connection to Selûne. Luna and his weapon blessing remained absent, and resting had not cured his shoulder injury. [00:18:21–00:18:32; 00:36:47–00:40:20]

Later, Osric explained his capture. Guards had seized a companion from their woodland camp; he remembered a hippogriff-like crest on their shoulders. When he demanded justice, they chained him, put him in the cart and sent him down the tunnel. He said the other knight was now dead. Osric offered to fight alongside them if armed. Vander lent him his trident, which Osric accidentally sent through the open window; Odettia recovered it on her return. [01:19:42–01:24:56; 01:36:36–01:36:48]

A bracelet and a broken peace

Quinn and Odettia met Isolde beneath a shaded tree near the farm. Quinn approached first with Sibbet’s bracelet. Isolde said a message from Voss had promised peace at a warehouse. She sent Sibbet, who never returned. Quinn described finding her on the chapel slab and gave Isolde the bracelet. She called it a kindness she would not forget, addressing him as brother. [00:48:20–00:55:39]

Isolde still described vampirism as a gift and saw potential in Odettia to lead a coven. She said Celandine had turned first: working as a night courier, her sister challenged a rogue vampire feeding on someone and was attacked. Later, Celandine offered Isolde the change. [00:55:42–00:59:34]

When Quinn and Odettia described their suspicion of poison, Isolde named Lily of the Shade, a white flower used to make poison that harms vampires. She said the governor’s greenhouse held the only specimen nearby. It was a new lead into Sibbet’s death, rather than proof of who poisoned her. [01:00:07–01:02:11; 01:43:07–01:43:30]

Celandine, Leon and the old governor

In exchange for a promise to arrange a meeting with Leon, Isolde answered their questions. She said Leon was born with vampiric blood that his body could not tolerate. Blood replacement, surgery and injections failed, leaving him ill for most of his life. Then his father died and Leon suddenly recovered. She now smells sulphur on him and doubts he is her nephew. The party explained oblexes to her; she had not known the creature by that name. Her suspicion does not settle what has happened to Leon. [01:03:00–01:07:40]

Isolde said Celandine was already a vampire when she married the old governor. He arranged their life around nighttime and secretly supplied prisoners for her to feed on. After years of failed treatments, Celandine wanted to take Leon elsewhere for care. According to Isolde, the governor discovered her plan and flew into a rage; Isolde found her sister staked on the dungeon floor, with guards behind her. Isolde holds the old governor responsible for Celandine’s death. [01:11:39–01:13:51; 01:17:33]

Quinn gave Isolde Celandine’s last letter. She wept as she read, her eyes lingering on “keep the boy safe.” After Odettia offered sympathy, Isolde touched her bitten neck and willingly released her bond. She told Odettia to find a priest for cleansing. The release was complete; the cure was not. Odettia still needed an item tied to Isolde and the Father’s rite, and intended to retrieve the gloves from Henrietta. [01:14:15–01:16:57; 02:03:55–02:04:24]

Isolde also identified the old governor as the payer of twelve years’ weekly blood shipments. She could understand a grieving father but could not forgive her sister’s killer. Asked whether she would fight if Leon had been taken over, she answered that she wanted to protect the true version of her nephew. She expected the promised meeting at dusk, at the farm, then left to join the mourning coven. [01:16:59–01:19:42]

Osric’s account of Umber and Sundar

At the farmhouse, Vander and Kaelith heard Osric’s account of Umber’s death. Masked attackers in blue cloaks bearing gold WSS letters ambushed the company. An arrow struck Umber in the neck; the survivors pursued his killers and their numbers dwindled. [01:24:56–01:28:22]

The key was returned to the Order by a member of the company before the later theft from the reliquary. Osric’s account made that sequence clear. [01:28:22–01:29:24]

Osric recalled records found while following the killers: the key suppressed an oblex’s ability to produce copies. This was his account of the records, rather than an effect the party had tested. [01:30:38–01:32:30]

He said Sundar entered a temple of Shar while pursuing the WSS. Years later, when they crossed paths again, Sundar bore a dark aura and shot one of their remaining companions through the skull. The others fled. Vander wondered whether this was still his brother; the explanation remained uncertain. [01:32:35–01:36:27]

Milo tests the door

When Quinn and Odettia returned, Milo tested his own ward by throwing a pebble at the front door. An enormous explosion blew away the door and frame and left the porch sagging. Milo stood soot-covered, his hair swept back. Kaelith used Elementalism to pour water over the fire, and Odettia cleaned him with magic. The farmhouse entrance was wrecked. [01:36:36–01:40:03]

The griffon’s answer

As the party considered how to reach Leon and the dungeon, Odettia felt the Griffontack grow warm. She went outside and called. A griffon descended: the same creature that had touched her amulet at the watchtower in the Fields of the Dead. She approached, bowed and touched it, feeling an immediate connection. Its help was temporary, discussed as lasting until midnight: a partnership with a willing creature. [01:44:00–01:48:36; 01:59:35–01:59:54; 02:04:40–02:04:51]

The plan took shape: Odettia and Kaelith would fly to the manor, try to seize Leon from the greenhouse or balcony and bring him to Isolde. Kaelith wanted to remain behind as a ladybird to seek the missing ingredient, with the others following by land toward town and the cave route. Communication and the exact approach were still being discussed. [01:49:10–01:55:23; 01:55:56–01:59:35]

Odettia mounted the griffon and pulled Kaelith up. Play paused as the raid was about to begin. The raid, abduction and dungeon return had not yet been carried out. [01:55:00–01:55:56]

Where things stand

  • Kaelith’s left forearm is missing, and the final ingredient for Corv’s remedy remains unrecovered.
  • Odettia has Isolde’s willing release, but still needs the cleansing rite and an item tied to her.
  • Osric is awake and the poison is removed. His testimony links Umber’s killers to the WSS and suggests a purpose for the key.
  • Isolde holds the letter and bracelet and expects a meeting with Leon at dusk.
  • The griffon has answered Odettia. She and Kaelith are mounted for the planned manor raid; the others intend to approach by land.
  • Halflin remains at large; Luna and the weapon blessing remain lost. Vander’s shoulder still needs treatment.
  • The farmhouse entrance is wrecked. The cellar blood scent, the cliff smoke and Sibbet’s final fate remain uninvestigated.
