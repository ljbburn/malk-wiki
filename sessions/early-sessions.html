Daggerford to the Fields of the Dead (Sessions 1–10)

Daggerford to the Fields of the Dead (Sessions 1–10)

07 Oct 20268 min read

These sessions were not recorded. This page is rebuilt from the DM’s own notes and the pictures shown at the table, so it is lighter than the later write-ups. If something here is wrong or missing, tell the DM.

Daggerford (Sessions 1–5)

The campaign opened on the first day of Tarsakh. Waterdeep had reinforced Daggerford because of rising tension with Amn, which feared an attack. The party answered a call for caravan guards (the poster is in the gallery) and took a contract from the tiefling merchant Priya Nagorth: escort her wagon of goods along the Sword Coast. Among the cargo was a strongbox stamped with the initials WSS. Priya did not know what was inside it.

Before the wagon left, each character spent a couple of hours preparing for the journey. The road out of Daggerford held several incidents: a flooded road, a woman feigning distress while her partner tried to rob the party, and a caravan inspection as they left the city. Beyond that, nothing survives in the notes.

Bearbarrow (Sessions 6–7)

The first night. Dusk found the wagon at Bearbarrow, a small village on the road, and the party took rooms at Isaac Townsend’s inn. Later that night they heard a sharp crash and a muffled scream from behind the tavern, where the wagon was parked. In the yard lay a shattered jar and the stable hand’s red scarf, with a trail of ooze and blood leading from the wagon to the village well. The session ended as something slipped into the well.

The morning after. Julius, the stable hand, walked into the tavern at breakfast, healthy and cheerful. The party did not believe it. They persuaded Constable Losandra Ayling that “Julius” was really an ooze creature wearing his face. Her deputy Bram Hallow reported that the gardener Edda Fenlow had not turned up for work. Her house looked ordinary, but a hidden path led from the cellar into an old mine. In the tunnel something pale and slick seized Bram and dragged him into the dark. The party chased it through a collapsing passage, fought the spawn of Julius and Edda in the first chamber, and climbed back out.

Rounding up the village. Back above ground the party worked with Losandra to account for everyone:

  • Ferris and Cade Holt, halfling brothers, sat in the village cell. They had been paid to start a disturbance at the inn that night so that Julius could reach the wagon unwatched. They did not know what was in the strongbox or what had happened to Julius afterwards.
  • Priya had gone through her papers and laid three documents on the inn table: her contract (marked with a stylised eye inside a circle, the WSS mark), a manifest for “one sealed research container, handle with care”, and a letter hidden in the strongbox’s false bottom, addressed to “the Society” and signed Aldric. It spoke of a “secondary site” being prepared. Priya, who had carried goods up and down the coast for years, asked: “Was I carrying something alive?”
  • Tomas Geld, the dwarf carpenter, was found at his farm with ooze on him. It slid off as the party came near. He had lost a whole day of memory, “like a floor where a staircase used to be”.
  • Denny, the baker, came when summoned and complained about the lack of walls. Sister Wren, a travelling healer with a cart in the square, had heard people running toward the well in the night.
  • Marta Voss had been replaced as well. The party and Losandra confronted the copy at the house on the west lane and destroyed it. Marta’s mother-in-law Old Petra Voss, bedridden, had known for two days that it was not her Marta. Losandra, who had known Marta for thirty years, told her gently that Marta was gone.

The night in the inn. Vander gave a speech to the gathered villagers, they raised their glasses, and everyone slept.

The second morning. Fresh horse tracks in the mud showed that a rider had sat at the south tree line for two hours in the night, watching the inn, then left before dawn. Bram woke and said only one thing: “Aldric. Tell someone: Aldric.” Minutes later Cassia Torn, Priya’s contact, rode in. She read the tracks as a scout, not a team, with the team half a day behind. She also gave the party a name and an address: Aldric Mourne, in Baldur’s Gate’s Outer City.

The mine. The party went back down through Edda’s cellar. The old Corwick silver mine ran down through an assay room full of forty-year-old records, a passage of alcoves where the oblex kept the stolen voices of the people it had taken, and a flooded shaft, to the oblex in its deepest chamber. Some of the voices in the alcoves were scraps of the party’s own memories. Cassia did not go down with them. The party destroyed the oblex with fire. Priya, shaken, decided to carry on to Baldur’s Gate to find Aldric Mourne, and Cassia travelled with them.

See oblexes-and-the-wss for what an oblex is and what the party knows of the WSS.

The road south (Session 8)

The wagon headed south with Priya driving and Cassia silent in the back.

  • A gnome with a cart stopped the party on the road. (He is the “gnome on the road” in the gallery; the DM does not remember his name.)
  • Morvin and Rilla Kettlebottom, halfling siblings who sell hand-dipped candles and soap between towns, sat on a boulder at a crossroads sharing bread and cheese while arguing about which inn on the road has the best beds. They tried to recruit the party to either side, and to sell them candles; Vander bought some.
  • Marvelo’s Magnificent Midway: a small carnival in a meadow off the road, with violet and gold wagons and lantern lines. Its ringmaster, Madame Zivra, a tiefling in a violet coat and a crooked top hat, could not stop making puns. Games cost 2 copper and paid brass prize tokens; the party played every game on the midway, including arm wrestling against a half-orc tent rigger (the “arm-wrestling orc” in the gallery).
  • In her tent Zivra gave Kaelith, Odettia and Vander the three prophecies. The wagon rolled on into the evening in near silence.

The Fields of the Dead (Session 9)

Gordo Plank, a weathered man with a mule named Duchess, sold “genuine artifacts of the Fields of the Dead”: rusty swords, a dented helmet and a bent arrowhead that he said had killed a famous general, each with its own grand story. When the party took him seriously he dropped the act and warned them: cross in daylight, stay on the road, never camp between the cairns, and if you hear voices, “it’s the wind”.

The Fields themselves are a vast, flat valley of cairns, the graves of the armies of Waterdeep and Amn that fought at the Winding River about two hundred years ago. One cairn is taller than the rest, with names carved in both languages. Cassia had crossed four times and never stayed for the night; Priya, six times, without looking.

On the road lay a dead ox with talon marks, a wrecked wagon, and a camp of stranded travellers at the ford led by Harsk, a retired Flaming Fist soldier. He said a young wyvern was nesting in the ruined watchtower beyond the crossing, and that the Fist patrols had been pulled into Baldur’s Gate, leaving the road unwatched. The party planned their approach, with Cassia’s crossbow in support, and killed the wyvern.

Odettia knew the iron perch rings on the tower wall: they were griffon fittings, the design her family uses at the Belabranta estate. After the fight a real griffon returned to its roost. It looked at Odettia, lowered its head, and let her stand near it. Her family amulet, the Griffontack, grew warm. The party crossed the ford and camped at the southern edge of the Fields, where Cassia mentioned a contact on the Baldur’s Gate docks named Harlan.

The ruins and Cassia’s briefing (Session 10)

The party reached eladrin ruins, almost entirely underground. Kaelith felt the traces of magic there were strong, and found writing describing the place as a gate. She also learned of tales of another crossing point, at the site of her ancient village, which is now held by the vampire lord (see cendriane). A woman named Thistle was there too. A drake ambush followed (the drakes and the cold camp are in the gallery), and during it a thief made off with the strongbox. The party never learned who it was and guessed the WSS. At the cold camp Cassia told them what she knew. The WSS was experimenting with oblexes in order to replace people of power. She had been investigating Aldric Mourne and had seven possible names for him, two of them the Holloway brothers. She also told them about the other oblex she had gone to investigate, and the sea cave north of Waterdeep where it was sealed. A pilgrim, Aldren Thace, gave Vander a letter for Brother Callum. From there the road led to Baldur’s Gate.
