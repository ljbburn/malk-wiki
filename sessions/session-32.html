Session 32: I Am Yours Until Midnight — Part 3

Session 32: I Am Yours Until Midnight — Part 3

18 Nov 20266 min read

Previous: Session 31. Times refer to the Session 32 recording.

What Leon owed Saltmere

Quinn brought Isolde to meet Leon at Harlan’s farm. She named herself his aunt and told him that his father had murdered Celandine for trying to take him away for treatment. Leon had grown up believing she died of illness. He said his own recovery came after something from Baldur’s Gate was injected into him, and that it had side effects. Asked if he was the creature Odettia had tracked to the island, he answered, “I am, and I am not.” [00:08:25–00:19:09]

Leon wanted to tell Saltmere his story and put the matter to its people. Odettia challenged his defence of feeding on prisoners. Leon said they had been sentenced to die and defended the arrangement as a cost he had accepted. Odettia doubted that all the prisoners were guilty and rejected his justification. Meanwhile two members of the coven tried to enlist Quinn in a hunt and offered him blood. He called their approach a betrayal of Isolde. [00:19:09–00:27:14]

Corv’s last word

At the Green Leaf, Milo told Vander he had meant to stop Halflin escaping. Vander urged him to learn to control his powers. Kaelith sent Barry to scout. The streets around the inn were quiet as most of the guards headed towards the farm. When Gerta announced a visitor under cover of housekeeping, Barry slipped through the front door and saw a hooded figure in a frost-covered coat. [00:27:25–00:39:08]

The visitor was Corv, with half his face already gone. He reached for Kaelith and vanished, leaving his robes and one final word: “Oblex.” Vander found a second vial sewn into the robes. It looked like Kaelith’s, and neither vial reacted. Kaelith took both and set out after Leon, convinced that Corv had named the missing ingredient. Vander and Milo followed on Luna, while Osric stayed at the inn to watch the guards. [00:39:43–00:47:07]

A signal and a flight to the caves

Leon told Isolde and Odettia he had an agreement with the creature and believed neither could live without the other. Overhead, the medic’s fireworks signalled for help. Odettia’s Suggestion barred magic against her and her allies, and the medic had found a way to call out while pinned beneath the griffon’s paw. Leon offered safety if they let him speak to his people. Odettia knocked him unconscious, hoping to gain time to find a way to separate them. Isolde carried Leon toward the coven’s seaside caves and gave the group an hour to come up with a plan. Odettia knocked out the medic too and brought him along. [00:54:15–01:06:43]

Vander and Milo rode around the marching soldiers and reached the farm as Odettia prepared to leave. Vander cleared up the garbled owl warning, then doubled back to collect Kaelith, who vaulted onto Luna at a run. Odettia and Quinn took the griffon, with Milo and the medic carried in its claws. Quinn felt the direction of Isolde through their blood bond and established a psychic link. Kaelith used Elementalism to hide the group’s trail. They stayed together and reached the firelit caves. Milo escaped the rough ride with a few cuts. [01:08:08–01:29:48]

The medic under the Zone of Truth

Odettia bound the medic as he woke and brought him before Isolde. She asked him to separate Leon and the oblex. Vander cast Zone of Truth through Luna, and the medic failed his save. He said Leon was the first person he had seen willingly make such an agreement. He suspected their souls had melded and was unsure what separation would do. [01:29:48–01:42:09; 01:53:46–01:55:53]

The medic admitted belonging to the Waterdeep Scientific Society. He said the WSS had two other oblex samples besides the one delivered to Saltmere. Their locations are unknown. His superiors saw a way to replace people for the benefit of Waterdeep’s ruling class. He identified the senior WSS member Aldric Mourne as Renard Holloway and said Holloway had left with the delivery ship. He also confirmed the strands in the dungeon came from Leon. The creature fed without leaving his body. [01:56:14–02:02:11]

The medic demanded safe passage off the island in exchange for more answers, and Odettia agreed. The WSS main laboratory is in Waterdeep. The medic described a specialised magical flame capable of killing an oblex, and warned that starving the creature might endanger Leon. He said the WSS had copied the Order of Aster key’s ability to suppress oblex replicas into other objects, some of which failed in transit. [02:11:23–02:13:16; 02:15:20–02:18:46]

Midnight

Leon woke in a panic and told the party that the oblex takes control at midnight. Dark wisps began to rise from him. The medic told Vander the key was around Leon’s neck. Vander cut him loose, and the medic ran. Leon warned that removing the key would make things worse. Odettia flew him to a patch of woodland away from town, set him down and withdrew. Kaelith sent Barry to watch. [02:23:13–02:36:16]

When the clocks struck twelve, the griffon landed, accepted Odettia’s thanks and flew away, fulfilling its bargain. Kaelith’s vial shook and pulled towards where Leon had been left. Quinn reached out through their psychic link and heard a voice almost like Leon’s. It cut him off. Through Barry’s eyes, Kaelith saw the figure straighten and look directly at the owl. Her link to Barry vanished. [02:36:17–02:41:54]

Kaelith cast Blur and started to retreat. A voice in the shadows told her, “Not you tonight, my darling,” and moved away. Odettia spotted an unidentified figure on the coast and sent Ariane to look. Vander mounted Luna to join her. Play paused before Ariane’s report. [02:42:29–02:46:43]

Where things stand

  • Leon was last seen in the woods, with the key still around his neck. The oblex has shown it can take control. The party has yet to find a way to separate them.
  • Kaelith is retreating with both vials and an ingredient lead. Her arm is still missing. Barry’s fate is unknown.
  • Odettia has roughly a day left for cleansing. The griffon has departed, and Ariane is investigating the coastal figure. [02:47:41–02:48:00]
  • The medic ran from the cave. His whereabouts and passage off the island remain open. Osric was last posted at the Green Leaf.
