Session 28: The Matter Is Closed

Session 28: The Matter Is Closed

15 Oct 20265 min read

Previous: Session 27

The orders behind the report

Odettia’s Clairvoyance caught a conversation in the manor office. A doctor said the dhampir’s blood had not clotted on a cloth; the other speaker ordered the sample kept. Odettia believed she was hearing Leon and his doctor, though the voices were not independently identified.

The conversation described a trap: a girl had taken a message into a shed, Voss had shut it behind her, and his men had killed her. The official account placed her beside her supposed victim. Orders followed to burn her body on the cliff before first light, with the arrows, without a crowd, and to stand the lanes down at three.

Milo’s father

Back at the Green Leaf, Vander’s healing gave the party a short rest. Milo sat unresponsive, silently moving his lips, surrounded by overwhelming magic.

Odettia tried Identify. She felt fear, grief and longing for family, then heard a distant voice: “This one’s mine.” She answered, “He is his own.” Her hand burned and she was thrown back. Milo woke confused and apologised for his “dad”. The party could not identify the presence.

Milo offered Odettia a drink he called a healing potion, stolen from a manor desk; it smelled of wine and did heal her. He also recalled two bodies on stone slabs, but could not retain the words of his experience. Exhausted and hungry, he stayed at the inn helping Gerta while the others investigated.

Sibbet and Hobb

At the guarded chapel, Kaelith became a ladybird and found a side medical room holding two bodies. Vander distracted guards with pirate rum while Quinn picked the rear lock and Odettia slipped inside.

A report named the dead woman Sibbet and the man Hobb. It alleged that Sibbet had stalked and drained Hobb before guards trapped her in a salt workshop and shot her through slits. A handwritten instruction called for Hobb’s body to be displayed as a warning about vampires.

Their examination challenged that account:

  • Hobb was human. His life force had been drained in a way unlike ordinary vampire feeding. The wounds on his neck were shallow, a knife lay on the slab, and dried, sulphurous residue coated his exposed skin.
  • Sibbet was a vampire the party recognised from the farm fight. Her mouth was clean of blood and residue. She bore the coven’s shared forearm tattoo.
  • Quinn remembered a chemical that could kill a vampire without a blow to the heart. Poisoned arrows and staged bite marks were the party’s explanation, not a confirmed finding.

Quinn took Sibbet’s metal, vine-like bracelet from her right wrist to show Isolde. The residue flakes were lost, not kept. The party closed and relocked the rear door.

The owls saw no activity on the cliffs. Ariane also showed Odettia a distant winged silhouette on the horizon; Odettia wondered if it was a griffon, but could not confirm it.

A prisoner transfer

The party returned to the cave entrance of the hidden passage. Quinn’s performance as a drunken intruder drew the guards’ attention, allowing the others through; he then escaped and climbed back into the tunnel.

Kaelith, still a ladybird, scouted an approaching cart. Two guards were transporting Osric, bound and gagged. Odettia disguised herself as a guard and presented Vander as another prisoner. Her bluff got him onto the cart beside Osric, with loose bonds he could escape. Kaelith rode underneath while Odettia and Quinn followed.

Beneath the manor

At about three in the morning, the cart reached the cells. About fifteen prisoners were connected to black, smoky tendrils that ran through cracks beneath a closed door at the corridor’s end. Kaelith thought of the Bearbarrow oblex, but these strands were more ethereal and less like ooze. What lay behind the door remained unknown.

When the guards tried to take Vander’s sword, he broke his bonds and attacked. Kaelith resumed her own form. The two escorts and two guards by the door fought them; Odettia and Quinn joined shortly afterwards.

  • Vander struck with Divine Smite, but a missed swing strained his right shoulder. He has disadvantage on Strength saving throws until the injury is treated; healing spells alone will not fix it.
  • Kaelith cast Spike Growth around the farther guards and used Land’s Aid to heal Vander. One prisoner could not remember his own name; another asked about his mother’s name.
  • Kaelith’s Produce Flame severed one prisoner’s tendril. Its detached end disintegrated, but the prisoner showed no immediate change.
  • Odettia’s Command made one guard flee for a turn; another resisted.
  • Crossbow bolts reduced Kaelith to 3 hit points and broke her concentration. Quinn landed a devastating psychic blade attack from hiding, then concealed himself again.
  • Vander reached Kaelith and restored 20 hit points with Lay on Hands, leaving her at 23.

Where play stopped

Combat is still underway. No guard was confirmed dead. Osric remains bound and gagged in the cart, and the prisoners remain in their cells. One tendril has been cut; the door is still unopened. Vander’s shoulder injury persists. The next round begins with Odettia.

Milo remains at the Green Leaf. Odettia’s bite is uncured, Quinn still holds Celandine’s letter and Sibbet’s bracelet, and no ingredient for Corv’s cure has been recovered. The order to burn Sibbet before dawn remains an urgent concern. See Open threads.
