Session 31: I Am Yours Until Midnight — Part 2

Session 31: I Am Yours Until Midnight — Part 2

18 Nov 20269 min read

Previous: Session 30. Next: Session 32. Evidence times use P1, P2 and P3 for the session’s three recordings; each part starts its own clock. Scenes switch between separated groups.

No link, and a parting signal

Quinn tried to extend his psychic link to cover the distance from the farm to the manor, but the attempt failed. The separated groups would have to manage without it. Vander fired an unusually impressive Sunbolt into the sky and felt a familiar, long-unheard presence humming at the edge of his mind. He wondered about his old Lathander training, but received no explanation. [P1 00:00:57–00:04:37; 00:10:37–00:11:44]

Vander, Osric and Milo set off by land. Quinn initially stayed behind. Odettia and Kaelith flew toward the manor on the watchtower griffon, disguising its riders with an illusion of sky. The griffon itself was still visible. [P1 00:12:04–00:15:43]

Ice through the greenhouse

Kaelith called Barry back from the cliff, sharing her distress and gratitude. Odettia sent Ariane ahead, then landed in a tree near the manor to scout through her familiar’s eyes. Guards noticed the perched griffon. Ariane saw Leon at his desk with the medic who had treated Quinn: a hand rested on Leon’s back, and magic flowed from the medic into him. [P1 00:18:35–00:22:56; 00:25:35–00:28:49; 00:32:10–00:32:56]

Odettia cast cold Dragon’s Breath on the griffon, its magic taking the shape of tiny icy pixies. It froze the glass, then crashed through the greenhouse roof. Leon and the medic turned amid the falling shards. Odettia used Suggestion to prevent the medic using magic against her or her allies, and the griffon seized both men. The spell replaced her concentration on Dragon’s Breath. [P1 00:35:08–00:44:40; spell clarified P2 00:28:24–00:31:37]

Kaelith leapt from the griffon, burned the greenhouse’s Lily of the Shade plants with Produce Flame and changed into a ladybird. Leon cried that those were all he had. The shattered greenhouse was left behind as Odettia prepared to escape with the captives. [P1 00:44:45–00:47:49]

A noble, his companions and a hooded guide

Vander hid his armour and red hair under a hood, asking Milo to call him “Uncle K.” Milo acquired a cloak for himself and an unexpectedly elegant disguise for Osric: pinstripe suit, top hat and monocle. Osric would pose as a noble leading bounty hunters into the guarded town. [P1 00:48:43–00:56:27]

Vander recruited a hooded rapier-wielder calling herself Julianne. After Osric talked and bribed their way through the checkpoint, she sold Vander a lead: Halflin might be found at the rope works by the docks after sundown. She accepted five gold for the information, in addition to the two he had given for joining them, while keeping her face concealed. As she left, she told him to give Halflin a message: his sister said hello. [P1 00:57:38–01:08:48]

The rope works looked deserted. Across the docks, four guards watched the separate warehouse containing the dungeon entrance. Vander and Osric decided to enter the rope works as prospective customers. [P1 01:10:43–01:12:31]

Quinn seeks help for the orb curse

Quinn crossed the wall by climbing a tree and visited Yara Dunwell. He described the headaches and the presence that returned more strongly each time he resisted it. He had not yet suffered gaps in his memory. [P2 00:00:07–00:01:50; 00:03:00–00:08:37]

Yara believed the curse threatened to separate spirit from body, leaving a spirit and an undead form if it overcame him. She could not remove it: he would need someone versed in soul magic, probably in a larger settlement. Her warning described what might happen, not a transformation already suffered. [P2 00:08:54–00:12:30]

She gave him an experimental three-dose mixture using herbs and preservation magic. A dose would help his next attempt to resist the orb within twenty-four hours, but would not cure it. She asked him to report what happened. Quinn also paid fifty gold for Poison of Silence, enough to coat about five arrows and interfere with a target’s magic; its detailed effects remained unexplored. [P2 00:12:44–00:20:51]

His attempt to recover money by picking a noble’s pocket went badly. A bodyguard caught and burned his wrist. Quinn tumbled free, failed to intimidate the man, and left for the meeting point. [P2 00:22:30–00:28:22]

Kaelith’s search

Inside the greenhouse, Corv’s vial was completely dormant. Kaelith searched anyway. A ledger listed unfamiliar names, dates spanning five years and tally marks under “cuttings.” In Leon’s study she saw a signed receipt from two weeks earlier for a “live specimen” and an “accompanying specialist.” Neither document explained what the terms meant. [P2 00:31:49–00:36:55; 00:39:28–00:41:35]

In the medical wing she found an undated observation chart for L. Thrace. Daytime entries described normal energy and bodily readings. The 12:30 a.m. entry read “agitated,” “violent,” “new powers?” Beyond it lay the familiar shackled bed and bloodstained floor. A partially patched opening still let her reach the hidden stair. [P2 00:43:00–00:48:48]

Kaelith passed at least thirty guards and new checkpoints underground as alarms began to sound. The cells had been scrubbed clean: no prisoners, smoke or severed tendrils remained. The door that had resisted the party was now ajar. [P2 00:50:10–00:52:13]

Beyond it stood two worn restraint chairs beneath open pipes, with bloodstains and thick residue on the stones. One person took notes while another cleaned with magic. They discussed an incoming transfer in two days, reviewing roughly ten or eleven prisoners and their alleged crimes with routine administrative detachment. They did not explain what would be done to them. [P2 00:54:35–00:58:47]

This was the room the vial had previously drawn her toward, but whatever it had reacted to was absent. Following sulphur-smelling residue through the pipes, Kaelith emerged into Leon’s ornate bedroom, then returned down the same route. She had found evidence and a connection between the rooms, but no ingredient and no answer to her failing spell. [P2 00:58:47–01:03:56]

Leon at the farm

Odettia distracted the guards with an illusory gelatinous cube and flew away. Three crossbow bolts missed. She descended into the trees and returned to Harlan’s farm, where Quinn rejoined her. Leon was set down; the griffon kept the suggested medic pinned. [P2 01:06:03–01:13:32]

Odettia told Leon that Isolde was his aunt and that his father had killed Celandine. Leon, overwhelmed, said his mother had died of sickness and denied knowing an aunt. In the cellar, Odettia illuminated the tapestry. He recognised his mother and her handmaiden, but did not accept their resemblance as proof of sisterhood. [P2 01:14:18–01:21:24]

Leon admitted that he already knew his mother was a vampire and that he was half-vampire. He still disputed the account of her murder. Asked how he had recovered, he said that they had found a cure. When Odettia named the oblex, he showed recognition but challenged why the cure’s nature mattered. He did not explain it. [P2 01:21:33–01:23:55]

He also mentioned seeing a man resembling Vander in town a couple of weeks earlier. Odettia suggested his brother, but that identification was not confirmed. After hearing about Celandine’s letter, Leon agreed to talk to Isolde in the hope of peace, even while doubting their kinship. [P2 01:20:17–01:20:54; 01:24:37–01:27:05]

Thunder at the rope works

When Vander’s scene resumed, his Sunbolt had knocked Halflin from the rafters. Vander tried to knock him unconscious, but Halflin scrambled away. Vander chased him through the rope works. Halflin protested that Vander would put him in chains after seeing what “that thing” had done to him. Vander offered the villagers’ judgement, then threatened chains or death. Halflin ran for the rear exit. [P2 01:27:14–01:32:08]

An enormous explosion destroyed the rear of the building. A voice told Vander “all has been restored,” and Luna returned, brushing against his leg. From beyond the wreckage, Milo explained that he had placed a trap after his “dad” suggested adding a little more power. He believed Halflin was dead; the fugitive’s body was not examined before they fled. [P2 01:32:08–01:34:38]

Vander gathered Osric, Milo came round to the front, and they escaped through a nearby pub. Three warehouse guards went to investigate the blast. The party tried to slip through the alleys, but Vander’s armour betrayed them and the guards gave chase. They stayed together, outpaced the pursuit and reached the Green Leaf, where Gerta hid them in a locked back room. [P2 01:34:47–01:37:26; P3 00:00:14–00:07:07]

The warning that went wrong

Kaelith emerged from the dungeon through the warehouse. A replacement orb-form guardian was waiting there, and additional carts bore the Gundwynd crest. She spotted Vander’s fleeing group, followed them to the Green Leaf and changed back inside the refuge. Luna’s return brought her first smile of the day, but the lost ingredient left her despairing. Milo asked his father for help and received none. [P3 00:07:19–00:13:44]

Through Barry, Kaelith saw about a hundred armed soldiers, some with magic, searching in the direction the griffon had flown. They did not know the farm’s precise location. She tried to warn Odettia and Quinn away from the dungeon and alert them to the danger. [P3 00:13:55–00:16:16; 00:24:34–00:25:19]

Barry found Ariane, but the urgent message became only “dungeon, urgent.” Odettia took it to mean their friends were in danger underground. Barry’s increasing panic made the misunderstanding worse. Odettia sent Ariane to check the surroundings while Quinn went to meet Isolde. No rescue journey had begun. [P3 00:25:19–00:27:58; 00:29:54–00:33:37]

Blood and a promise

At sunset Isolde arrived with about twelve members of her coven, explaining that she had prepared for an uncertain meeting. Quinn told her Leon was there, and she acknowledged the party had kept its agreement. [P3 00:31:11–00:32:43; 00:34:59–00:35:30]

Calling Quinn a true ally, Isolde bit her hand and offered a pact to look out for one another. Quinn reciprocated. Their blood mingled as they shook hands, and he felt a bond form. Isolde promised that she and her coven would be there if he needed them. [P3 00:35:43–00:37:10]

Quinn and Isolde walked toward Leon. Play paused immediately before their conversation. [P3 00:37:10–00:37:23]

Where things stand

  • At the farm: Odettia, Quinn, Leon, the medic and the griffon; Isolde and her coven have arrived. The family meeting is about to begin.
  • At the Green Leaf: Kaelith, Vander, Milo, Osric and the returned Luna, sheltered in Gerta’s back room.
  • The search force is moving toward the farm’s region. Its arrival and the party’s response have not happened.
  • Luna and Vander’s blessing are restored. Halflin is apparently dead after Milo’s trap; the other escapees remain unresolved.
  • Kaelith’s forearm remains missing, the vial dormant and the ingredient unrecovered. The manor records and pipes are new leads.
  • Quinn has temporary help for the orb curse and Poison of Silence, neither used during this session, and a new bond of mutual aid with Isolde.
  • Odettia still needs cleansing. Leon’s cure remains unexplained, and no peace settlement has been reached.
