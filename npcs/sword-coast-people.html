Daggerford and Bearbarrow

Priya Nagorth


A tiefling merchant who hauls goods along the Sword Coast. The party escorted her wagon out of Daggerford. Something in her cargo, a strongbox marked WSS, turned out to be far more dangerous than she knew.

Losandra Ayling


A tall orc, the constable of Bearbarrow and its only law. The party convinced her that the stable hand had been replaced by an ooze creature. Odettia has written to her and is still waiting for a reply.

Isaac Townsend


The jovial red-haired dwarf who keeps Bearbarrow’s inn.

Julius


Bearbarrow’s teenage stable hand, with a red scarf. He was taken and replaced by the oblex.

Bram Hallow

Losandra’s deputy. A creature dragged him into the mine beneath Edda Fenlow’s house, and he woke the next morning insisting that someone must be told the name Aldric.

Edda Fenlow

A Bearbarrow gardener who vanished from her farm the morning after the wagon arrived. Her cellar hid a path into the old mine.

Ferris and Cade Holt


Halfling brothers and petty criminals, held in Losandra’s cell. They were paid to cause a disturbance at the inn so that Julius could reach the wagon, and they swear they knew nothing more.

Tomas Geld, Denny and the Voss family

Tomas Geld is Bearbarrow’s dwarf carpenter, who lost a whole day of memory to the oblex. Denny is the baker, who wants proper walls. Marta Voss was replaced by a copy, which the party destroyed. Her mother-in-law, Old Petra Voss, had known for two days that it was not Marta.

Sister Wren


A travelling healer who arrived in Bearbarrow a few days before the wagon, selling remedies from a cart in the square. She heard people running to the well in the night.

The road south

The gnome on the road


A gnome with a cart, met on the road south of Bearbarrow. His name is not recorded.

Morvin and Rilla Kettlebottom

Halfling siblings who sell candles and soap along the Sword Coast, and argue about which inn has the best beds. Vander bought candles from them.

Zivra


The fortune-teller at the carnival who gave three of the party their prophecies (S8).

Gordo Plank


A relics dealer on the road into the Fields of the Dead, whose mule Duchess will not cross after dark. His relics are fake, but his warning about the Fields was not.

Harsk

A retired Flaming Fist soldier turned turnip farmer, stranded at the ford with other travellers by a wyvern (S9).

Cassia


A former Waterdeep City Watch investigator, Cassia Torn, who rode into Bearbarrow at Priya’s call and travelled south with the party. Briefed the party on the WSS (S10). Her letter at sea (S16) narrowed “Aldric Mourne” down to four men, two of them the brothers Erstin and Renard Holloway.

Aldren Thace

His letter reached the party in S10, and Vander delivered it to Brother Callum in Baldur’s Gate.

Baldur’s Gate

Theron Vale


The Belabranta family steward, a half-elf with a military bearing, and Odettia’s childhood tutor. He posted a 500 gp reward for her. She confronted him in disguise (S11), and the party later watched the Black Boars threaten him (S14).

Orin Castellar


Owner of the Closed Door, an invitation-only antiques showroom in the Upper City. Through him the party learned Sundar sold the Order of Aster key (S13). Kaelith bought a Cendriane emerald there.

Others

  • Bessa Ironhand: keeper of the Lizard’s Cradle, the party’s base, where Vander holds the stew record (8 bowls).
  • Thalwick: bookseller on the Basilisk Gate, who sold the party a book (S15).
  • Brother Callum: Shrine of the Moonmaiden.
  • Harlan of the Loose Rigger: a harbour-bar contact, and not Saltmere’s farmer.
  • Dane Copperweight: a jeweller whose cellar the party cleared (S14). It connects to the sewers, and he now believes someone matching Odettia fled down there.
  • Edren Gale: a wizard who teleported to the Black Boars’ hideout (S14).
  • Crane: a Black Boars figure.
  • Marloes: Milo’s mother.

At sea

Reza Sahil

The pirate whose three contests the party took on (S18): a drawn duel, the befriending of Biscuit, and a card game Odettia won. The prizes included a replacement chart and the greatsword Marianne, taken from Ser Callum Voss.
