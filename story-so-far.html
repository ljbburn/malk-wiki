The Story So Far

The Story So Far

07 Oct 20264 min read

Daggerford and the wagon (Sessions 1–5)

The adventure began in Daggerford, a town reinforced by Waterdeep amid rising tension with Amn. The party took work escorting the wagon of the tiefling merchant Priya Nagorth. There are no recordings from these early days; see early-sessions for what the DM’s notes preserve, and the gallery for the map of Daggerford and the poster that started it.

Bearbarrow (Sessions 6–7)

On the road, Priya’s wagon stopped at the village of Bearbarrow. Something escaped from her cargo in the night: an oblex, an ooze that steals memories and wears people’s faces. It replaced the stable hand Julius. The party exposed the impostor to the constable, cleared the mine beneath the village, and found a strongbox marked WSS.

The road south (Sessions 8–10)

At a carnival the fortune-teller Zivra gave Odettia, Vander and Kaelith each a prophecy. The party fought a wyvern, and at an old watchtower Odettia’s family amulet, the Griffontack, woke. In eladrin ruins Kaelith found a crossing point toward her lost home, Cendriane. During a drake ambush, a thief made off with the WSS strongbox; the party guessed the WSS but never learned who. Afterwards Cassia briefed the party on the WSS.

Baldur’s Gate (Sessions 11–15)

In Baldur’s Gate they found Odettia’s face on a reward poster put up by her family’s steward, Theron Vale. They traced the WSS’s local operative, Aldric Mourne, to an Outer City building and a theatre, the Vellum Stage. There they rescued young Milo and overheard the name Holloway.

At an antiques house called the Closed Door, Vander learned the worst: his brother Sundar had sold the stolen Order of Aster key. The buyer’s agent called himself “Mourne”, and the key left west aboard a ship, the Tideworth. Someone signing only “M.D.” had written asking about it too, and was heading for Baldur’s Gate.

The party dodged the Black Boars gang hunting Odettia and laid a false trail. They freed Milo’s mother from a Guild debt, then sailed at dawn on Captain Neve Saren’s ship, the Maris, after the Tideworth.

The voyage (Sessions 16–22)

A storm nearly took Kaelith, whose body began to fade, and Neve went overboard and was rescued. Sahuagin raided the ship by night, leaving strange spiderweb coins. Someone burned the chart and fouled the compass. A stowaway, Quinn, was found in the hold.

The pirate Reza Sahil challenged the party to three contests, and they came away with a new chart and the greatsword Marianne. The coins led them to a drowned Waterdeep navy wreck, its dead crew, and a kraken spawn they barely escaped. At the dark Viledel lighthouse they destroyed a dread wight and a dread wraith and relit the beacon. Quinn looked into the lighthouse’s orb and has carried a curse ever since.

Saltmere (Sessions 23–27)

They reached Saltmere, a harbour town in the Moonshaes, just as its old governor was buried. His son Leon Thrace threw out the nobles, seated the commoners and declared a celebration.

While Odettia ran the funeral from inside, Quinn and Milo slipped into the manor. They found Leon’s old sickroom, with shackles on the bed, and a hidden stair down to a dungeon with fifteen chained prisoners. Vander and Kaelith destroyed a guardian construct in the harbour storehouse that the tunnel leads to.

The next day the party split up for jobs around town, and every thread pointed back in time:

  • a hunter who arrived decades ago and never left
  • a thirty-year order letting manor wagons pass the docks unchecked
  • harbour “wine” that was really blood
  • a governess murdered twelve years ago
  • the handmaiden Isolde, who vanished at the same time

At Harlan’s farm Vander destroyed the farmer’s risen corpse. Kaelith met Corv, a man fading out of time as she is. And Leon invited them to dinner.

Next they found a vampire coven living under Harlan’s farm, saved the Order of Aster knight Osric from it, and struck an uneasy truce.

At the governor’s table Leon asked them to destroy the coven, for 800 gp. That same night they learned the coven’s leader is Isolde, sister of Leon’s mother Celandine, and that she blames the manor for her sister’s death. See Session 27.
